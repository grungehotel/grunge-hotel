STEMS are multitrack audio files for live-stage (especially) or studio performance. It separates individual instruments of mix so you can control to rebalance/remix again. Some livebands/orchestras can`t perform fully-sympho\hardly-electronic music, so there I go. I`m recreating that stuff by ear with Studio One DAW note to note, sound to sound, sample to sample. Its not a stealing, it`s a replication following author`s ideas, tricks and hardware treatment. 

All multitracks are 24 bit 44.1 KHz WAV files in 1 archieve. Click Track included, Master File included if needed.

BIO

Electronic music producer, multi-instrumentalist, audio engineer, DJ, coach from Kazakhstan, Almaty.
He started learning music at the age of 5 on piano, earning money at the age of 11. In 2K the journey into Digital Audio Workstations has begun. New technologies of PC era and VST`s brings some opportunities to start get into production secrets, not just sitting around and pressing keys on your old bread-and-butter workstation.
Nowadays, ReFil is continuing to working hard on his sound and skills at the Studio215 with Ramil Aliev about ELSP & Soul Engineers  projects. Check it Out!
Co-producer of Omnisphere, ElectroSpirit, ELSP, Soul Engineers projects. Feedbacks and support from Russian Size Group, DJ Boyko Soundshocking, Markus Schulz, Joachim Garraud, Graham Gold, Darin Epsilon, Justin Testa, Chicane, Hans Zimmer, Jaytech, Kris O`Neil, Cosmic Gate, Anhydrate, Roger Shah, Dash Berlin, Kiss FM, DFM.
Movie and Theater music production, Orchestral and Cinematic production, advertising, Live Band Playbacks, arrangement, ghost-producing and remixing.
